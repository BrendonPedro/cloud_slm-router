## 3. Experiments: what was and was not run

**Run (measured, raw evidence on disk).**
- Stage 0 (iteration 12): routing labels were built from **LLMRouterBench's
  released per-query scores**, with no model inference by us. Two pool pairs
  (P-L, P-Q) were evaluated in-domain over 5 seeds and in 4 leave-one-dataset-out
  (LODO) folds each. The baselines were always-edge, always-cloud, random,
  dataset-prior, TF-IDF+LR (implemented in the standard library) and oracle,
  with 10,000-resample bootstrap confidence intervals. The command log is
  `experiments/prototype/logs/commands.txt` and the raw output is
  `results/stage0_raw.json` (sha256 `3f83fff0…`). A re-run gave byte-identical
  predictions and splits (`logs/determinism.txt`).
- Headline, as summarised in `results/stage0_summary.md`: in LODO, TF-IDF+LR was
  never significantly better than random on deferral AUC (0/8 folds) and was
  significantly worse in 3/8. In-domain on P-Q it significantly beat
  dataset-prior in only 2/5 seeds. These are **baseline** numbers on **someone
  else's labels and model pool**, not the manuscript's pool.

**Not run (anything stating otherwise is an error).**
- No micro-encoder, Tiny-LM, discriminative encoder (option B), self-routing
  SLM (option C) or structured decision model (option D) was trained. The
  blocker was that no pip or torch was available on the host.
- No Jacobi Forcing training or decoding was run. No speed-up was measured by us;
  the 2.57× and 3.97× figures in deliverable 6 are the authors' reported numbers.
- No Split-LLM partition was deployed. No activation transfer, latency, energy
  or privacy leakage was measured.
- No SimpleQA inference was run. The manuscript's edge and Split-LLM models
  have **no** SimpleQA records in LLMRouterBench (claim-audit E2), so the
  manuscript's data plan needs new inference runs.
- Jev/System One was not called. Everything in deliverable 5 is vendor claims
  or third-party repositories.
- `paper/main-revised.tex` was **never compiled**, because there is no TeX on
  the host.

## 4. What was machine-checked, and the limits of that

The checkers confirm **internal consistency**, not truth. They were written by
the same agent that wrote the text, so they share its blind spots.
- `check_bib.py`: 101 entries are well formed, rebuild byte-identically from the
  saved primary records, and cover every cited ID.
- `check_paper.py`: the source sha is unchanged; the 72 cite keys resolve; the
  tables match a fresh regeneration from the raw JSON; banned result phrases are
  absent.
- `check_brief.py` / `check_talking_points.py`: every factual anchor appears in
  the deliverable it is attributed to; the Stage-0 counts are recounted; venue
  labels follow library.bib entry types.
- `check_sprint_report.py`: all 18 deliverables exist and this report's anchors
  trace to their sources.
