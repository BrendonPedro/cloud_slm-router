- It is **structurally complete**: abstract, introduction, related work,
  formulation, candidate designs, protocol, preliminary measurement, results
  placeholder, limitations and conclusion.
- It is **experimentally incomplete**. No proposed router has been trained or
  measured. §VII "Results and Discussion" is a `\todo{}` placeholder and must
  stay empty until raw outputs exist.
- The **only measured numbers** are the Stage-0 simple baselines in §VI, which
  were computed from LLMRouterBench's released per-query scores with no model
  inference. Tables II–III are generated by `make_tables.py` from
  `experiments/prototype/results/stage0_raw.json`, and every number in the §VI
  prose is checked against the generated tables and the summary.
- All planned work is in the future tense and marked `\todo{...}`. Advisor
  decisions (title, Jacobi scope, replacing the strong model, thresholds) are
  marked `\todo{Advisor: ...}`.

## Commands

```bash
python3 paper/check_paper.py    # consistency checks; exits non-zero on any failure
```

`check_paper.py` (stdlib only) verifies:
- the original is unchanged;
- all 72 `\cite` keys resolve to `references/library.bib`;
- the `\input` files exist and match a fresh regeneration;
- every §VI prose number appears in the raw-derived evidence;
- the bootstrap counts quoted in §VI (LODO 0 better / 3 worse than random; in-domain 5/4 seeds better than random and 2/2 better than dataset-prior) recompute from `stage0_summary.md`;
- banned result phrases ("extensive experiments", "novel", "state-of-the-art", …) are absent, and "without retraining" or "outperform" appear only in attributed or negated sentences.
